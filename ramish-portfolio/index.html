How I run a campaign

  1. 1

    Understand

    What are we trying to achieve? Whether it's sales, leads, or brand awareness, we define what winning looks like first.

  2. 2

    Target

    Who are we trying to reach? Mapping customer intent, studying what competitors are running, and finding the right segments.

  3. 3

    Build

    Setting up the campaign structure, budget rules, audiences, and ad creatives cleanly in Meta Ads Manager.

  4. 4

    Test

    Launching structured variations to see which hooks and visual formats stop the scroll and earn clicks.

  5. 5

    Learn

    Looking at CTR, CPC, and frequency numbers to figure out what's working and why.

  6. 6

    Improve

    Refining winning ads and pausing underperformers instead of randomly changing campaign settings.
